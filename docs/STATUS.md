## 2026-11-18 (night) — HDMI-CEC TV-remote passthrough SHIPPED (`airbridge-cecd`)
- New package `openwrt/packages/airbridge-cecd` (source `media/cecd/`): daemon claims CEC logical address 4 ("AirBridge", Playback 1) on `/dev/cec0` (PA 2.0.0.0 from the TV EDID), broadcasts `Active Source`, answers `Request Active Source`, and maps LG Magic Remote keys to `airbridge.media` (select=toggle, left/right=minus/plus 10 s, up/down=volume -/+5, back/stop=stop). Installed, enabled and running on the device; deployed binary sha256 42a7418a21b30abd988a1aad0d87dcd0639625e2a0de08657f0b46e3f7dd2a32. User confirmed live on the C4 — pause/resume, seek and volume all reacted — with server-side verification agreeing (six `User Control Pressed` receptions logged, dispatched key codes and exact volume arithmetic matched).
- Root cause of the zero-receive behaviour: the 6.12 CEC framework queues received messages **only to follower filehandles** (`cec_receive_notify` queues to `adap->cec_follower`); claiming logical addresses is not enough, and with no follower the kernel feature-aborts everything directed at us. Fixed with `CEC_S_MODE(INITIATOR|EXCL_FOLLOWER)`. Related traps recorded in MEDIA.md: `vc4_hdmi` has no monitor capability (`cec-ctl -m` lies unless it claims a logical address itself), a killed CEC client wedges its LAs until `cec-ctl -C` or reboot, and webOS routes remote keys only to its active source.
- `CEC Standby` and `Image View On` were verified ad hoc (the C4 powered off and back on from the Pi during development); automatic TV power coupling deliberately not wired in — the user chose passthrough only.
